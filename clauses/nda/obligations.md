{{ receiving_party }} shall limit disclosure of the Confidential Information to those of its directors, employees, and professional advisers who have a genuine need to know it for the Purpose, and who are bound by written obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those contained in this Agreement. {{ receiving_party }} shall remain liable for any breach of this Agreement by such persons.

{{ receiving_party }} shall promptly notify {{ disclosing_party }} in writing upon becoming aware of any unauthorised use, disclosure, or loss of the Confidential Information, and shall provide reasonable assistance to {{ disclosing_party }} in mitigating the effects of such unauthorised use or disclosure.

Where {{ receiving_party }} is required to disclose Confidential Information by law, by a court of competent jurisdiction, or by a regulatory authority, it shall, to the extent legally permissible, give {{ disclosing_party }} prompt written notice so that {{ disclosing_party }} may seek a protective order, and shall disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information which it is legally compelled to disclose.

Upon the written request of {{ disclosing_party }}, or upon termination of this Agreement, {{ receiving_party }} shall promptly return or irretrievably destroy all Confidential Information in its possession or control and shall certify such destruction in writing.

Nothing in this Agreement grants {{ receiving_party }} any licence, right, title, or interest in or to any Confidential Information, or any intellectual property of {{ disclosing_party }}.
