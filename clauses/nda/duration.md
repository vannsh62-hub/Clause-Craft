This Agreement shall come into force on {{ effective_date }} (the "Effective Date") and shall continue in full force and effect for a period of {{ duration_years }} years, expiring on {{ term_end_date }}, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party.

The confidentiality obligations set out in this Agreement shall survive the expiry or termination of this Agreement and shall remain binding upon the parties for the period specified in the Confidentiality clause.
