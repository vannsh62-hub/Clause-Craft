During the term of this Agreement and for a period of {{ duration_years }} years thereafter, {{ receiving_party }} shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment any employee of {{ disclosing_party }} with whom {{ receiving_party }} came into contact in connection with the Purpose.

This clause shall not restrict {{ receiving_party }} from employing any person who responds to a general advertisement or recruitment campaign not specifically directed at the employees of {{ disclosing_party }}.
