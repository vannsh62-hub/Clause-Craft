{{ receiving_party }} shall hold all Confidential Information disclosed by {{ disclosing_party }} in strict confidence and shall not disclose such Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of {{ disclosing_party }}.

{{ receiving_party }} shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever. {{ receiving_party }} shall not commercially exploit, reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble any Confidential Information.

{{ receiving_party }} shall protect the Confidential Information using no less than a reasonable degree of care, and in no event less than the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature.

The obligations set out in this clause shall survive for a period of {{ duration_years }} years from the date of disclosure of the relevant Confidential Information, notwithstanding any earlier termination of this Agreement.
