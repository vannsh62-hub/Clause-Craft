{{ service_provider }} shall provide to {{ client }} the following services (the "Services"): {{ services_description }}.

{{ service_provider }} shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with generally accepted professional standards, and in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations.

Any change to the scope of the Services shall be agreed in writing by both parties before the affected work is undertaken. No variation shall be binding unless recorded in a written change order signed by an authorised representative of each party.

{{ service_provider }} shall perform the Services as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship between the parties.
