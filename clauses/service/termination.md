This Agreement shall commence on {{ effective_date }} and shall continue until the Services are completed, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this clause.

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving {{ notice_days }} days' prior written notice to the other party.

Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party: (a) commits a material breach of this Agreement which is incapable of remedy; (b) commits a material breach which is capable of remedy and fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of written notice requiring it to do so; or (c) becomes insolvent, enters liquidation, or has a receiver or administrator appointed over any of its assets.

Upon termination, {{ client }} shall pay {{ service_provider }} for all Services properly performed up to the effective date of termination, and {{ service_provider }} shall deliver to {{ client }} all work product for which payment has been made.

Termination shall not affect any rights, remedies, obligations, or liabilities of the parties that have accrued up to the date of termination.
