In consideration of the Services, {{ client }} shall pay {{ service_provider }} a fee of {{ currency }} {{ fee_amount }}, exclusive of applicable taxes.

{{ service_provider }} shall submit invoices to {{ client }} in respect of the Services. {{ client }} shall pay each undisputed invoice within {{ payment_days }} days of the date of receipt.

All amounts payable under this Agreement are exclusive of Goods and Services Tax and any other applicable taxes, duties, or levies, which shall be paid by {{ client }} in addition at the prevailing rate.

Where {{ client }} disputes an invoice in good faith, it shall notify {{ service_provider }} in writing within ten (10) days of receipt, setting out the grounds of the dispute, and shall pay the undisputed portion in accordance with this clause.

Interest shall accrue on any overdue undisputed amount at the rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower, from the due date until payment in full.
